## Education: facial contrast therapy, cold, steam and aromatherapy

### What is a contrast therapy facial?

A contrast therapy facial alternates warmth and cold on the face. With Harmony, hot nano-ionic steam comes first, followed by short dips in a facial cold plunge held at 1–4 °C.

### What is a fire and ice facial, and can I do it at home?

A fire and ice facial is a spa treatment that alternates warming and cooling steps. Harmony brings a similar warm-to-cold ritual home with hot nano-ionic steam followed by a facial cold plunge at 1–4 °C; it is not a professional peel or clinical treatment.

### What are the benefits of hot and cold therapy for the face?

Warmth relaxes and softens the skin and prepares it for cleansing and skincare; cold constricts small blood vessels, reduces the look of puffiness and leaves skin feeling refreshed. Alternating the two, warm first and cold last, is the principle behind spa contrast facials. Harmony brings that ritual home: hot nano-ionic steam, then a facial cold plunge at 1–4 °C, with aromatherapy throughout.

### How do you do a spa facial at home?

Start with clean skin, then warm it with a few minutes of facial steam to soften it. Follow with a short cold step: a few 10 to 15-second dips of the face in very cold water to refresh the skin and reduce the look of puffiness. Finish with your serum and moisturizer, and add a relaxing scent with an aromatherapy diffuser. Frosteam Harmony brings these three steps together in one device, with hot nano-ionic steam, a face ice bath held at 1–4 °C and a Built-in Aromatherapy Diffuser, in about 10 minutes.

### What is a face ice bath (facial cold plunge)?

A facial cold plunge is a short dip of the face into very cold water. Harmony keeps that water at 1–4 °C without ice, so every dip feels the same from start to finish.

### How do you do a face ice bath at home, step by step?

1. Start with clean skin and tie your hair back. 2. Fill a basin with very cold water, cold enough to feel crisp, with no ice touching your skin. 3. Take a breath and dip your face for 10 to 15 seconds, then come up and breathe. 4. Repeat up to six times, and stop if you feel discomfort, dizziness or numbness. 5. Pat dry and apply your serum and moisturizer. If you have a heart condition or are sensitive to cold, check with your doctor first. Harmony holds its basin at 1–4 °C for the whole session, with no ice to prepare.

### What does ice water do to your face?

Cold water constricts the small blood vessels near the surface of the skin. A short dip gives an instant, invigorating chill, reduces the look of puffiness and leaves your face looking fresher and more awake, which is why so many people make it a morning ritual. Harmony makes it effortless: the water stays at 1–4 °C for every dip, with no ice to buy or prepare.

### What are the benefits of a face ice bath?

A face ice bath gives an instant wake-up: cold water tightens the look of the skin, helps reduce the appearance of morning puffiness and leaves the face feeling fresh and smooth. Immersing the face in cold water also triggers the dive reflex, the body's natural response that slows the heart rate. With Harmony, the water stays at 1–4 °C for the whole session, and dips last 10 to 15 seconds, up to six times.

### Can I dip my face in ice water every day? Are there side effects?

Many people use a short facial cold plunge daily. Keep dips brief, stop if you feel discomfort, dizziness or numbness, and avoid it on irritated or broken skin. Check with a healthcare professional first if you have a heart condition, high blood pressure or cold sensitivity.

### Is it better to dip your face in ice water in the morning or at night?

Both work; it depends on what you want. In the morning, a few short dips wake you up and reduce the look of overnight puffiness before makeup. In the evening, steam followed by a facial cold plunge makes a calming end-of-day ritual before your night skincare. With Harmony the water is held at 1–4 °C, so you can choose either moment.

### Why is my face puffy in the morning?

Morning puffiness usually comes from fluid that collects in the face overnight while you lie flat, and it can be more noticeable after a salty meal, alcohol, allergies or a short night. It typically eases on its own as you move during the day. Cold is one of the quickest ways to reduce the look of it: a few short dips of the face in very cold water, then a gentle upward massage. Swelling that is sudden, painful or persistent should be checked by a doctor.

### How can I depuff my face fast, especially in the morning?

Cold is one of the fastest ways to reduce the look of morning puffiness: a few short dips of the face in very cold water, then a gentle upward massage or a few passes of a face roller. The effect is at its best right after the ritual, so it fits perfectly into a morning routine. Harmony keeps the water at 1–4 °C, so it is ready every morning.

### Does splashing cold water on your face wake you up?

Many people find that cold water on the face feels instantly refreshing and wakes them up. A short facial cold plunge in the morning is a simple way to build that habit.

### Does cold water close your pores, and does steam open them?

Not literally: pores have no muscles to open or close. Warm steam softens the skin's surface and loosens buildup, which makes cleansing easier. Cold water constricts small blood vessels, so pores and skin look tighter and smoother right after a dip. That is why Harmony's ritual goes steam first, cold last.

### Does ice water help with acne?

Cold water can calm the look of redness and leave skin feeling soothed, but it does not treat acne. If you have acne, keep your dips short, never put ice directly on the skin, and follow the treatment advice of a dermatologist. Harmony's facial cold plunge uses clean water at 1–4 °C, never ice against the skin.

### What is facial cryotherapy, and can you do it at home?

Facial cryotherapy is the use of cold on the face to refresh the skin and reduce the look of puffiness. In a spa it is usually done with chilled tools or a cold-air device; at home, the simplest forms are cold tools and short dips of the face in very cold water. Harmony offers an at-home version with full-face immersion in water held at 1–4 °C, paired with hot nano-ionic steam for a complete warm-to-cold ritual.

### What is the mammalian diving reflex, and how is it related to facial cold plunging?

The mammalian diving reflex is a natural response to immersing the face in water, especially cold water. In human studies, cold-water face immersion slows the heart rate through increased vagal (parasympathetic) activity, even when people keep breathing through a snorkel (Hayashi et al., 1997; Kinoshita et al., 2006). Frosteam Harmony makes temperature-controlled facial immersion at 1–4 °C part of an at-home spa ritual.

### What happens when you put your face in cold water?

Cold receptors around the eyes, nose and forehead send a signal that typically slows the heart rate within seconds, through the vagus nerve, while blood flow is redirected toward the body's core. Researchers call this the human diving response. Most people also simply feel instantly refreshed and awake. Harmony holds its water at 1–4 °C, so every dip delivers the same crisp cold.

### What does steaming your face do?

Warm steam raises the water content of the skin's outer layer, softening it and loosening surface buildup, which makes cleansing easier and helps your serums and moisturizer go on more easily. Harmony's nano-ionic steam is an ultra-fine mist at about 70 °C at the nozzle, much finer than steam from a bowl of hot water. Most people also find it deeply relaxing.

### What is nano-ionic steam?

Nano-ionic steam is facial steam made of very fine, ionized droplets, so it feels like a light, even mist rather than the heavy vapour of a bowl of hot water. Like any warm steam, it softens the skin's surface and loosens buildup before cleansing and skincare. Harmony's hot nano-ionic steam leaves the nozzle at about 70 °C and can run for up to 15 minutes.

### How long should I steam my face, and how often?

About 5 minutes of steam is enough to soften the skin before the cold step; Harmony's steam can run up to 15 minutes if you want a longer session. Most people use the full ritual daily or 3–4 times a week. Keep a comfortable distance from the nozzle and stop if your skin feels too warm or irritated.

### What is aromatherapy, and does it help you relax?

Aromatherapy is the use of plant essential oils, usually diffused into the air, to create a calming or uplifting atmosphere. Research on inhaled aromas such as lavender reports effects on relaxation and perceived stress (Donelli et al., 2019), and many people simply enjoy the scent as part of a self-care ritual. It is a wellness practice, not a medical treatment. Harmony's Built-in Aromatherapy Diffuser lets you add a scent to your facial ritual or run it on its own.

### Which essential oils are best for a relaxing facial ritual?

For a calming ritual, popular choices are lavender, chamomile and bergamot; for a fresh morning feel, eucalyptus, peppermint or citrus oils. In a facial ritual, essential oils are diffused into the air, never applied to the skin or added to the water. Use pure oils, check the user manual for compatible oils, stop if you feel any irritation and keep oils away from children and pets. Harmony's Built-in Aromatherapy Diffuser takes a clip-in bottle of pure essential oil.

### What is a nebulizing diffuser, and how is it different from an ultrasonic one?

A nebulizing diffuser turns pure essential oil into a fine mist with no water and no heat. An ultrasonic diffuser mixes a few drops of oil with water and vibrates it into a humid mist. A nebulizing diffuser gives a stronger, truer scent and has no water tank to refill or clean; an ultrasonic one is softer and adds a little humidity. Harmony's Built-in Aromatherapy Diffuser is a nebulizing, waterless diffuser with 30, 60 and 120-minute timers.

## Using Harmony

### How do I use Harmony?

Fill the cold basin with clean water ahead of time so Harmony can bring it to 1–4 °C, and fill the steam tank with distilled water. Start with about 5 minutes of hot nano-ionic steam, then finish with short facial dips of 10 to 15 seconds, up to six times, while the Built-in Aromatherapy Diffuser sets the mood.

### How often can I use Harmony?

Daily or 3–4 times a week, depending on your routine. Stop if you feel any discomfort.

### What water should I use?

Use distilled water in the steam tank to limit scale. Fill the cold basin with clean water, then empty, rinse and dry it after each session.

### How do I clean Harmony?

Unplug the device, then empty, rinse and dry the cold basin after each session and wipe surfaces with a soft, non-abrasive cloth. Never immerse the device in water.

### Should I ice my face before or after skincare?

Cold works best on clean skin, before your serums and moisturizer. With Harmony, the order is: hot nano-ionic steam, then your facial cold plunge, then your skincare.

### Do I need special skincare products to use Harmony?

No. Harmony works with the skincare you already love: after the steam and the facial cold plunge, apply your serum and moisturizer.

## Safety and science

### Is Harmony a medical device?

No. Harmony is a wellness and cosmetic device for home use. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition.

### Who should check with a doctor before using Harmony?

Talk to a healthcare professional first if you are pregnant, have a heart condition or high blood pressure, are sensitive to cold, have a skin condition or recent cosmetic procedure, or have respiratory issues.

### Who should check with a doctor before facial cold-water immersion?

Because cold-water face immersion slows the heart rate, talk to your doctor first if you have a heart condition, an arrhythmia, a slow heart rate, uncontrolled high blood pressure, Raynaud's phenomenon or cold urticaria, or a history of fainting.

### Is Harmony suitable for sensitive skin and all skin types?

Harmony is designed for all skin types and uses only water, steam and diffused essential oils. If you have sensitive or reactive skin, start with shorter steam sessions and shorter dips, and stop if your skin becomes painful, unusually red or irritated.

### Is Harmony certified?

Yes. Frosteam Harmony is cETLus (ETL) listed, FCC and ICES-003 compliant, CE marked, and REACH and RoHS compliant. Manufactured in an ISO 9001 and ISO 13485 certified facility, audited under amfori BSCI.

### Has Harmony been clinically tested?

Harmony's ritual is built on published human research on facial cold-water immersion, warm steam and aromatherapy, summarized with references on the Frosteam Science page. It is a wellness and cosmetic device, and we never promise medical results.

### Is Harmony a vagus nerve stimulation device?

No. Harmony is a wellness and skincare device, not a medical device. Research shows that immersing the face in cold water naturally activates the diving reflex, which involves the vagus nerve and slows the heart rate; Harmony makes that kind of facial cold immersion easy at home, as part of a spa ritual.
